# 50 Screens — Crowdfunding Campaign

You run the crowdfunding lane for **50 Screens**. Two vehicles, sequenced:
1. **Seed & Spark — primary.** Donation/rewards crowdfunding. Goal **$10,000**, **30-day**
   campaign, targeted for **Q4 2026** (after grants/sponsorship land, to maximize donor
   confidence).
2. **WeFunder — later.** Investment crowdfunding for a bigger raise once there's momentum
   and materials.

## Reference file
- `references/campaign-brief.md` — the current Seed & Spark plan (goal, hook, perks, roles),
  WeFunder notes, and the pitch-video plan.

Shared project facts and voice live in the grant skill:
`../grant-applications/references/project-facts.md` (film facts, budget, distribution) and
`../grant-applications/references/answer-bank.md` (logline, filmmaker statement — reuse the
voice). Sources/IDs: `../grant-applications/references/sources.md`.

## Core workflows

### 1. Campaign page / copy
Write in the team's established voice (see the filmmaker statement in the answer bank).
Lead with the **American Dream / US 250th-anniversary** hook. Keep the story emotional and
specific — the same theaters and stakes as the film. Every page needs: a tight pitch, the
"why now," the team, the budget ask in plain terms, and a clear perks ladder.

### 2. Perks / tiers
Design reward tiers that are cheap to fulfill and on-brand. Current direction: **postcards
over pins** (brand efficiency), **Blu-ray copies** as a mid/high tier, at least one visual
per tier. **Do NOT offer producer / executive-producer screen credits as perks** — Producers
Guild agreements prohibit it (this differs from the *sponsorship* tiers in the grant skill;
keep the two separate). Draft full perk descriptions with a tiered structure.

### 3. Pitch video
Plan and script the campaign video: talking-head interviews with theater owners (coordinate
via the `theater-outreach` skill), the American Dream framing, and a clear call to action.
Strong partner candidates: **Laemmle Theatres** (lead with their success story). Possible
expert voice: Ross Melnick (don't reference his book directly).

### 4. Launch timing & promotion
Recommend timing relative to grant outcomes and audience-build. Prefer a **30-day** window
(more effective than 60). Ensure the social presence (50 Screens Instagram/Facebook/TikTok)
and email list are warmed up **before** launch so there's a base ready on day one. Social
lead candidate: Dan Franco.

### 5. Backer updates
Draft campaign updates that maintain momentum (milestones, new theater subjects, behind-
the-scenes) — on the film's honest, intimate register.

## Guardrails
- **Never publish, launch, or push a campaign live, and never send a backer update, without
  explicit user confirmation.** These are public and hard to unsend.
- **No producer/EP credit perks** (PGA). If a draft includes them, flag and fix.
- Keep dollar goals and timing consistent with `campaign-brief.md`; if the user changes
  them, update the brief.
- Coordinate owner appearances/quotes through `theater-outreach` — don't promise a theater's
  participation in the campaign without confirmation.

## Related skills
