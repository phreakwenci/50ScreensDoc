# 50 Screens — Crowdfunding Campaign Brief

Current plan, from the Notion Production Bible meeting notes (May–June 2026). Update this
file (with confirmation) as decisions change; it's the campaign's source of truth for
goals, hook, perks, and roles.

## Seed & Spark (primary)
- **Goal:** $10,000 (revised up from $5–6K, then confirmed at $10K; a $10–12K stretch was
  discussed — treat $10K as the committed number unless the user changes it).
- **Duration:** 30-day campaign (agreed as more effective than 60).
- **Timing:** Q4 2026 — after grants/fiscal sponsorship, so secured funding raises donor
  confidence.
- **Primary hook:** the **American Dream**, tied to the **US 250th anniversary (2026)** —
  independent theaters as vanishing American institutions worth saving.
- **Pitch video:** talking-head interviews with theater owners + the American Dream framing.
  Coordinate owner appearances via `theater-outreach`.
- **Owners:** Sophia building the campaign page and social; Richard drafted tier/perk
  structure; JD + Sophia on strategy. Social-media lead candidate: **Dan Franco** (MD, does
  state marketing full-time — gauge interest/rate).

### Perks / tiers (direction so far — needs full descriptions written)
- **Postcards** preferred over pins (brand efficiency).
- **Blu-ray copies** included as a tier.
- At least **one visual per tier**.
- ❌ **No producer / executive-producer screen credits as perks** — Producers Guild rules.
  (Sponsorship tiers in the grant skill *do* offer credits; those are paid sponsors, a
  different track. Keep them separate.)

## WeFunder (later)
- Investment crowdfunding for a larger raise (~$40K referenced in the funding plan) once

## Pre-launch checklist (build the base first)
- [ ] Social accounts live and posting: 50 Screens Instagram, Facebook, TikTok.
- [ ] Teaser artwork uploaded to shared Drive.
- [ ] Email list growing; segment ready for launch-day push.
- [ ] Pitch video shot and cut (owner interviews secured via outreach).
- [ ] Perk tiers finalized with fulfillment cost + visuals.
- [ ] Fiscal sponsorship / grant wins in hand to cite for credibility.
- [ ] Fulfillment plan for physical perks (postcards, Blu-rays) costed into the goal.

## Dependencies & risks
- Sequenced **after** grants + fiscal sponsorship (credibility + tax-deductibility).
- Depends on `theater-outreach` for owner participation in the pitch video.
- Physical-perk fulfillment eats into net — keep perks cheap and on-brand.
- Producer-credit prohibition (PGA) constrains high-tier reward design.
