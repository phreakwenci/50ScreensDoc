## Fit heuristic
Strong fit = documentary + development/early-production stage + one or more of {cultural
preservation, community/place, arts, regional (CA/MD/GA/MI or "American" scope)} + either
no fiscal-sponsor requirement OR sponsorship in place. Down-rank: fiction-only, completion
funds requiring a locked cut, region-locked funds outside the team's states, funds needing
a prior feature-doc directing credit the team doesn't have, or funders whose eligibility
window (e.g. "within 3 months of post-production") the project doesn't meet yet.

## Where to hunt for new opportunities
Prioritize funders matching the eligibility profile above; the tracker above already
surfaced many good ones (JustFilms, Southern Documentary Fund, Berkeley Film Foundation,
Firelight, Chicken & Egg). Additional starting points (verify current cycles with
`WebSearch`):
- **Documentary funder databases:** IDA (documentary.org); The Film Collaborative; From
  the Heart Productions' own resource newsletter (already subscribed — mdlafilms@gmail.com
  gets it monthly, worth skimming for their "Grant Finalists" / funding-opportunity content).
- **National doc funds not yet screened:** Fork Films, Perspective Fund, Field of Vision,
  Doc Society, Sundance Institute's other programs (Art of Nonfiction, etc.).
- **Cultural-preservation / humanities:** state humanities councils (Maryland, California,
  Georgia, Michigan), NEA, American Archive / historic-preservation grants.
- **Identity-aligned:** Native/Indigenous funds (e.g. Vision Maker Media — verify
  eligibility for the project vs. the individual), Latino/a media funds for Sophia-led
  angles — **cross-check eligibility carefully** given the Chicken & Egg / Firelight
  lesson above (gender/race eligibility rules vary by program).
- **Regional / state film offices & arts councils:** CA (WEHO, city/county arts), Maryland
  (MD Film Office, Maryland State Arts Council), Georgia, Michigan.

When proposing matches, for each give: **funder · amount/type · deadline · eligibility
fit (incl. fiscal-sponsor requirement) · one-line why-it-fits-50-Screens**, and rank by
fit × deadline urgency. Then offer to add the strong ones to the budget-sheet tracker.
