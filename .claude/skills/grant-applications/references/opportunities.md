## Fit heuristic
Strong fit = documentary + development/early-production stage + one or more of {cultural
preservation, community/place, arts, regional (CA/MD/GA/MI or "American" scope)} + either
no fiscal-sponsor requirement OR sponsorship in place. Down-rank: fiction-only, completion
funds requiring a locked cut, region-locked funds outside the team's states, funds needing
a prior feature-doc directing credit the team doesn't have, or funders whose eligibility
window (e.g. "within 3 months of post-production") the project doesn't meet yet.
