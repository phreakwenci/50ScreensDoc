## Eligibility profile (screen every candidate against this)
- **Project:** feature documentary (~90 min), US production, in **development / early
  production** (principal photography 2026; completion Jan 2028).
- **Fiscal status:** independent; **fiscal sponsorship not yet secured** (targeting IDA or
  The Film Collaborative). Many funders require a 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor — flag those
  as blocked until sponsorship lands. Note funders that **don't** require it (e.g. NEH).
- **Budget:** $614,451 (a **sub-$500K** version exists for funders that cap budgets).
- **Team/identity levers** (use where a funder prioritizes them): director **Jan-David
  Soutar** is Native American / **Eastern Band Cherokee** heritage; producer **Sophia
  Hernandez** is **Latina** and a cinematographer; team is **LA + Maryland/DC**-based;
  first-time feature-doc director with prior short/narrative credits.
- **Themes that unlock thematic funders:** American cultural preservation, small business
  and economic change, community and place, working-class life, the arts, media/exhibition
  history.
- **Craft levers:** observational / vérité; theatrical-first distribution innovation
  (Nouveau Roadshow); community-embedded access.
- **Constraints:** no WIP footage until mid/late 2026 (blocks WIP-gated funds like ITVS
  Open Call until then); non-union; original score / no licensed music (clean for E&O).

## Fit heuristic
Strong fit = documentary + development/early-production stage + one or more of {cultural
preservation, community/place, arts, regional (CA/MD/GA/MI or "American" scope)} + either
no fiscal-sponsor requirement OR sponsorship in place. Down-rank: fiction-only, completion
funds requiring a locked cut, region-locked funds outside the team's states, or funders
whose max budget or eligibility the project can't meet.

## Where to hunt for new opportunities
Prioritize funders matching the profile above. Good starting points (verify current
cycles with `WebSearch`):
- **Documentary funder databases:** IDA (documentary.org) funding/resources; Documentary
  Filmmakers' funding guides; The Film Collaborative; From the Heart Productions (Roy W.
  Dean).
- **National doc funds:** Sundance Documentary Fund, ITVS (Open Call / Diversity
  Development), Chicken & Egg Pictures, Catapult Film Fund, Points North, Fork Films,
  Perspective Fund, Ford/JustFilms, Field of Vision, Doc Society.
- **Cultural-preservation / humanities:** NEH (Media Projects), state humanities councils
  (Maryland, California, Georgia, Michigan), NEA, American Archive / historic-preservation
  grants (fits the "vanishing American institution" angle well).
- **Identity-aligned:** Native/Indigenous funds (e.g. Vision Maker Media — verify
  eligibility for the project vs. the individual), Latino/a media funds for Sophia-led
  angles.
- **Regional / state film offices & arts councils:** CA (WEHO, city/county arts), Maryland
  (MD Film Office, Maryland State Arts Council), Georgia, Michigan — often overlooked and
  less competitive.
- **Labs & fellowships (career + access, some cash):** Film Independent (Doc Lab, Fast
  Track, Amplifier), IFP/Gotham Week Project Market, Points North Fellowship, BAVC
  MediaMaker, Firelight, Catapult's programs, Sundance labs.

When proposing matches, for each give: **funder · amount/type · deadline · eligibility
fit (incl. fiscal-sponsor requirement) · one-line why-it-fits-50-Screens**, and rank by
fit × deadline urgency. Then offer to add the strong ones to the tracker.
