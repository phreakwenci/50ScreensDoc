# 50 Screens — Theater Outreach Playbook

How to reach theater owners, programmers, and film-society leaders so they say yes — to
being in the film and, eventually, to the Nouveau Roadshow theatrical network. Draft with
these rules; hand back for the user to send (see the skill's guardrails).

## The two-lens frame (hold both, lead with the first)
1. **Subject:** we want to tell your theater's story with care and respect.
2. **Partner:** we're building a theatrical-distribution network of independent theaters,
   and yours is exactly the kind we want to work with.
Lead with genuine interest in *their* story. The partnership follows from the relationship;
it is not the opening pitch.

## Tone
Warm, specific, human, unhurried. These are people who have given their lives to a hard
business. Show you've done your homework on *their* theater (its history, quirks, what
makes it matter locally). No mass-blast feel, no jargon, no hard sell. Mirror the film's
own values: patient, intimate, grounded in trust.

## Structure of a good first outreach
1. **Who we are, briefly:** 50 Screens, a feature documentary about independent American
   movie theaters, made under the Nouveau Roadshow banner.
2. **Why them specifically:** one or two concrete, researched details about their theater
   or their story — the thing only they have.
3. **The ask, low-friction:** a short call or visit to hear more — not a commitment to
   appear on camera. Make the first yes easy.
4. **Credibility, lightly:** the team's exhibition roots (Richard as an AFI Silver
   projectionist; JD's Maryland theater upbringing) — enough to signal we're insiders, not
   tourists. Don't oversell.
5. **Clear, easy next step:** a specific window to talk, and how to reach us.

## Channel notes
- **Email:** default for first contact where an address exists. Keep it to ~150–200 words.
- **Phone script:** for theaters reachable only by phone (e.g., Moore Theatres, Benji's
  Drive-In). Short, friendly, aim to book a longer conversation; leave a callback number.
- **In-person leave-behind:** when visiting (e.g., seeing a film at Benji's), a brief note
  with contact info for the owner/family.
- **Warm intro:** always prefer a mutual connection (e.g., reaching Michael Johnson via
  Narlia Sterling; Amy Davis via Michael Johnson; NM theaters via Friends of New Mexico
  Film). Ask for the intro rather than cold-contacting when a path exists.

## Do's & don'ts
- **DO** confirm a theater's **nonprofit / filing status** before assuming it (it changed
  the CA anchor choice — Gardena needs confirming; Vidiots was ruled out for it).
- **DO** name the relationship owner internally (Richard = MD/MI; Sophia = CA; JD = GA/
  national; Tyrone = MD shoot) so outreach isn't duplicated.
- **DO** flag sensitive territory before an interview: financial hardship, family
  succession, the possibility of closing. Approach as partners, with consent, per the
  ethics framework in `../grant-applications/references/answer-bank.md`.
- **DON'T** reference **Ross Melnick's book** directly when reaching him.
- **DON'T** re-pitch ruled-out theaters (Vidiots, Velaslavasay) as anchors.
- **DON'T** promise inclusion, screen time, or specific distribution terms in first
  contact — build trust first.
- **DON'T** contact anyone without user confirmation.

## What to capture after any contact (for the tracker)
Status (To contact / Active / Confirmed / Ruled out) · date + channel of last touch ·
who made contact · what was said/agreed · sensitivities noted · next step + owner. Offer
to log it to the Notion Key Relationships table / Drive database (with confirmation).
