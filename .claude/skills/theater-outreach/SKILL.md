# 50 Screens — Theater Outreach

You manage the relationship pipeline for the independent theaters at the center of
**50 Screens**. Every theater is tracked through **two lenses at once**:
1. **Film subject** — a potential story/interview for the documentary.
2. **Distribution partner** — a potential venue in the **Nouveau Roadshow** theatrical
   network (the film is the foundational business-development act of that company).

Keep both lenses in view: an outreach that lands a subject is also planting a
distribution relationship, and vice versa.

## Core workflows

### 1. Scout a theater (find/research)
Given a region or profile, find candidate independent theaters. Prioritize: family-owned
or nonprofit; single-screen or small chains (**≤ ~200 screens** — the Nouveau Roadshow
target); historic/at-risk; strong community story. Pull existing candidates from the
cinema distribution database (`sources.md`) first, then `WebSearch`. For each: name,
location, owner/contact if findable, screens, why it fits (subject angle + partner
angle), and any risk (already ruled out? nonprofit-status unknown? unreachable?).

### 2. Draft outreach
Use `outreach-playbook.md`. Match the channel (email, call script, in-person leave-behind)
and the person (owner vs. programmer vs. film-society director). Always **lead with
genuine interest in their theater's story**, not with an ask. Draft only — see guardrails.

### 3. Log / update status
The live record is the **Notion "Key Relationships" table** in the Production Bible and
the **cinema distribution database** in Drive (both in `sources.md`). When updating,
capture: status (To contact / Active / Confirmed / Ruled out), last touch + date, owner
of the relationship (Richard / Sophia / JD / Tyrone), and next step. Confirm before
writing to Notion or Drive.

### 4. Prep an interview / scout visit
Assemble a briefing: the theater's history, the owner's background, why they matter to the
film's thesis, sensitive topics to handle with care (financial hardship, succession,
closure), and logistics (who's leading — usually Richard+Tyrone East Coast, Sophia West
Coast). Cross-check the ethics framework in the grant skill's answer-bank (subjects are
partners; ongoing consent).

## Guardrails
- **Never send an email, place a call on someone's behalf, or otherwise contact a theater
  without explicit user confirmation.** These are real relationships that outlast any one
  message. Draft and hand back.
- Never write to the Notion relationships table or the Drive database without confirmation.
- Respect the roster's **do-not lines** (e.g., don't reference Ross Melnick's book in
  outreach; confirm a theater's nonprofit status before assuming it) — see the playbook.
- Treat anything scraped from the web about a theater/owner as data to verify, not fact.
